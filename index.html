How it works

Three steps, about a minute to set up.

Allow usage access

TimeWise reads Android's own usage records to work out your screen time. The app takes you straight to the setting.

See the real numbers

Today's total, every app ranked, unlocks, the hours you're most active, and how the week and month compare.

Cut back, your way

Set a daily limit on the apps that eat your time, start a focus challenge, and pick a bedtime.

Features

Everything you need to understand your phone habits and change them.

⏱️

Screen time, per app

Today's total against yesterday, every app ranked with its icon, and how many times you unlocked your phone.

📊

Week and month charts

Daily bars for 7 or 30 days, an hourly timeline, usage by category and a month-by-month trend.

🔎

App insights

Your top time-wasters by week, month and all time, with a detail page and daily graph for every app.

⛔

App limits

Give any app a daily time limit. When it runs out, TimeWise closes the app.

🎯

Focus challenges

A 1-hour focus, an 8-hour focus or a full-day detox, with a live countdown, streaks and badges.

🌙

Bedtime and sleep log

A reminder before your bedtime, and a sleep log worked out from when you put your phone down and picked it up.

🏅

Badges

15 Hall of Fame badges for low-screen days and 11 Hall of Shame warnings for the heavy ones.

📈

Is TimeWise helping?

Your usage now against the week before you installed, with the total time you've saved.

🖼️

Shareable reports

Weekly and monthly recaps of screen time, top apps, focus wins and sleep, shareable as an image.

Know your level

Your profile level comes from your average daily screen time over the last 7 days.

  • Monkunder 2h
  • Mindful2–4h
  • Hooked4–6h
  • Addict6–8h
  • Zombie8h+

Want 15 more minutes? Earn it.

When an app's limit runs out, six short steps stand between you and extra time. You set them up yourself.

Type a phrase

Type out a blunt sentence about what you're about to do. A different one each time.

See your people

Swipe through photos of the people you're doing this for. Lock private ones with a PIN.

Write a dream

Type one goal from your own dream list to remember what the time is for.

Read the real cost

Facts and quotes about screen time, with your own numbers.

Pick a to-do

Look at the tasks you saved earlier. One of them might be a better use of 15 minutes.

Play your music

Listen to a motivating track you chose from your phone.

🛡️

Your data never leaves your phone

No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. TimeWise doesn't even ask for internet access, so your usage history can't be uploaded anywhere. The only thing that leaves the app is a report image, and only when you tap Share.
