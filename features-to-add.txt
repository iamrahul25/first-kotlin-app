Features added: 
--------------

- Profile section (Home header pill / Settings > Your profile): 5 levels from the 7-day daily
  average - Monk (<2h), Mindful (2-4h), Hooked (4-6h), Addict (6-8h), Zombie (8h+). Shows a
  gauge of where you stand, how much to cut a day to level up, days a year lost to the phone,
  and the full level ladder.

- "Is TimeWise helping?" card (Profile screen): saves your average from the 7 days before
  install as the starting point (or your first week if Android has no older data), then shows
  before vs last 7 days, a verdict (working / no change / worse), total time saved, unlocks
  before vs now, weekly chart against the starting point, and milestones: install date and
  when usage access and notifications were turned on. Settings shows "With TimeWise since".

- App insights (Stats > App insights): Week / Month / All time ranking of the biggest
  time-wasters with total, average a day, days used and trend vs the period before, plus a
  "#1 time-waster" card. Tap any app (here, or in Home / Stats / Apps lists) for its detail:
  time lost so far, today, this week (vs last week), last 30 days, daily average, a 7 / 30 day
  daily graph, days opened, busiest day, share of screen time and days a year at this pace.
