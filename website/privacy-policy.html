Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: TimeWise reads your phone's app usage so it can show you your screen time, and it keeps that information only on your own device. The app has no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not have permission to use the internet. We never receive, store, share or sell any of your data.

This Privacy Policy explains how the TimeWise app for Android (the "App", package name com.nightwise.timewise), developed by Rahul Kumar ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information. It also covers this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. There is no account to create and no sign-in of any kind.

The App does not declare the Android internet permission, so it cannot send your usage data, device identifiers or anything else to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives data from the App.

2. Information the App accesses on your device

To do its job, the App reads some information that Android already keeps on your phone. All of it is processed on the device and stays there.

Information How the App uses it
App usage history: which apps were in use and for how long, and when the screen was turned on, off and unlocked To show total and per-app screen time, unlock counts, the hourly timeline, charts, badges, your profile level, reports and the sleep log
Names and icons of the apps installed on your phone that have a launcher icon To label apps in the usage lists and let you choose apps for daily limits
The app currently open on screen (only if you turn on the accessibility service) To enforce the daily app limits you set and to show the floating focus timer. See section 5
Photos you pick Shown back to you as one of the extra-time steps. A scaled-down copy is saved in the App's private storage
Audio files you pick Played as one of the extra-time steps. The App keeps a link to the file, its title, artist and cover art. The audio itself is not copied

Photos and audio are chosen through Android's own picker, so the App can only see the specific files you select. It has no access to the rest of your gallery or storage.

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves the following in its private storage on your phone. None of it leaves your device unless you choose to share a report (see section 6).

Data Why it's stored
Daily summaries: total screen time, unlock count, your most used apps and sleep times for each day Android keeps detailed usage records for only a short time, so the App saves a daily summary to show month views, trends and reports
Your starting point: average usage from the week before you installed the App, the install date and when each permission was turned on To show whether TimeWise is helping, and your milestones
Focus challenges, streaks and badges earned To track your progress
App limits you set and the extra time you've earned To enforce your limits
Extra-time content you add: photos and captions, your dream list, to-dos, and your chosen music tracks To show them to you when an app's limit runs out
Photo PIN, stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value To keep photos you mark as private hidden until the PIN is entered
Settings: bedtime schedule, app categories you changed and other preferences To keep the App set up the way you like it

We cannot see, access or recover this data. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, it is permanently removed from your phone.

4. Permissions

  • Usage Access (required): lets the App read Android's usage records to work out screen time, per-app time, unlocks and the sleep log. You grant it yourself in your phone's settings and can turn it off at any time.
  • Notifications (optional): for the bedtime reminder, focus challenge results and countdown, and app limit alerts.
  • Accessibility service (optional): needed only for app limits and the floating focus timer. See the next section.
  • Run at startup: lets the App set your bedtime reminder and nightly save again after your phone restarts.

The App never uses your camera, microphone, location or contacts, and it has no internet access.

5. Accessibility service

TimeWise includes an optional accessibility service, which you can turn on in your phone's Accessibility settings if you want to use app limits. It is used only to:

  • notice which app is open in front, so the App can count time against the daily limit you set;
  • close an app and show your extra-time steps when its limit runs out;
  • show a small floating timer over other apps while a focus challenge is running.

The service is set up so that it cannot read what is on your screen or what you type. It only receives the name of the app that comes to the front. This information is used on your device at that moment and is never sent anywhere. You can turn the service off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings; the rest of the App keeps working.

6. Sharing reports

You can share a weekly or monthly report as an image. This happens only when you tap Share. The App creates the image and hands it to Android's share sheet, and you choose which app or person receives it. The image shows the summary on the report (such as screen time totals and your top apps). What happens to it afterwards is covered by the privacy policy of the app you shared it with.

7. Device backups

If you have turned on Google backup for your phone, Android may include the App's saved data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is a feature of the operating system, controlled by you in your device settings and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to these backups.

8. Third parties, ads and analytics

  • The App shows no advertising.
  • The App contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone, because we don't collect any.

The App is distributed through Google Play and GitHub. Those platforms may collect information when you download the App, under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover them.

9. Children's privacy

TimeWise is not directed at children under 13. Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Deleting your data

Since there are no accounts and nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. You can erase everything the App has saved at any time by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete Account & Data page.

11. Security

Your data stays in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Because the App has no internet access and no data is transmitted to us, there is no risk of it being exposed from our servers. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the App, so keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

12. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads its font from Google Fonts, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add an optional sign-in or online backup in the future), we will update this policy before that change is released and update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
