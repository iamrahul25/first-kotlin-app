Delete Account & Data

TimeWise, by Rahul Kumar · Last updated: November 14, 2026

TimeWise has no accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in, so there is no account to delete. We don't keep any of your data on a server, and the app has no internet access. Everything (your screen time history, limits, badges, photos and settings) is saved only on your own device, and you can erase it yourself at any time using the steps below.

What data exists

The only data TimeWise keeps is stored locally on your device:

  • Daily summaries of your screen time, unlocks, most used apps and sleep times
  • Your starting point from the week before install, and when you installed the app and granted permissions
  • Focus challenges, streaks and badges
  • App limits and extra time earned
  • Extra-time content you added: photos and captions, dreams, to-dos, chosen music tracks and the photo PIN
  • Your settings (bedtime schedule, app categories and so on)

We never receive any of this, and we hold no personal information about you. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

How to delete your data

Choose whichever option suits you. Deleted data can't be recovered.

Option 1: Clear the app's storage

Erases everything TimeWise has saved and keeps the app installed. It starts fresh next time you open it.

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → TimeWise.
  3. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage).
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

Menu names vary slightly between phone makers and Android versions.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling TimeWise removes the app and all of its saved data from your device. Press and hold the app icon and tap Uninstall, or uninstall it from the Google Play Store.

Option 3: Remove individual items

Inside TimeWise you can remove single photos, dreams, to-dos and music tracks from the extra-time setup, and remove the limit from any app, without clearing everything else.

Stop TimeWise reading your usage

You can also take back the app's access without deleting it:

  • Usage Access: phone Settings → Apps → Special app access → Usage access → TimeWise, then turn it off.
  • Accessibility service: phone Settings → Accessibility → TimeWise, then turn it off.

Android's own usage records belong to your phone, not to TimeWise, and stay in place after the app is removed. Android clears them on its own over time.

Copies you made yourself

  • Shared reports: if you shared a weekly or monthly report image, it was sent to the app or person you chose. Delete it from that place (for example, the chat you sent it to).
  • Device backups: if Google backup is turned on, your phone may have backed up the app's data. You can manage or delete these backups in your device or Google account settings. We don't have access to them.
  • Original photos and music: removing them from TimeWise does not delete the original files from your gallery or storage.

Requests to the developer
